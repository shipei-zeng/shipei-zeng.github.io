Bio
======
Shipei Zeng is an Associate Professor at the [Shenzhen Research Institute of Big Data](http://sribd.cn/en/teacher/320). His research spans the digital economy, digital government, and computational social science, with a focus on economic measurement and artificial intelligence. He also serves as an Adjunct Scholar at the [School of Humanities and Social Science, CUHK-Shenzhen](https://hss.cuhk.edu.cn/en/page/1350), an Advisor to the Center for AI for Social Science at the [Shenzhen Loop Area Institute](https://www.slai.edu.cn/en/home), and a Digital Government Expert for the Shenzhen Government Services and Data Management Bureau. He has been recognized as a Guangdong Big Data Talent and selected as a Shenzhen Pengcheng Peacock Talent. He holds BEcon and MEcon degrees from Renmin University of China and a Ph.D. in Economics from UNSW Sydney.

Dr. Zeng uses quantitative economic analysis and computational methods to study AI applications in government services, efficiency statistics, and data-driven risk assessment. He has 20+ publications, including work in the *Journal of Forecasting*, *Computational Economics*, *International Review of Finance*, and *Information and Software Technology*. He is the developer of [dfvad](https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/dfvad/index.html), an open-source R package officially distributed through CRAN, with 18,000+ downloads.

He has led or contributed to national, provincial, and municipal research projects and directed 10+ industry-sponsored projects. His work applies generative-AI evaluation, explainable AI, and ensemble learning to public administration, finance, energy, and telecommunications, supporting government large-language-model evaluation, economic monitoring, and applications in anomaly detection, credit assessment, anti-fraud verification, and intelligent financial analysis.

曾诗培，广东省大数据工程技术人才、深圳市鹏城孔雀人才，现任[深圳市大数据研究院](http://sribd.cn/teacher/320)副研究员，兼任[香港中文大学（深圳）人文社科学院](https://hss.cuhk.edu.cn/page/1350)客座学者、[深圳河套学院](https://www.slai.edu.cn/zh-hans)社会科学智能中心特聘顾问、深圳市政数局数字政府专家。中国人民大学经济学学士、硕士，澳大利亚新南威尔士大学经济学博士。主要研究领域为数字经济、数字政府、计算社会科学。围绕经济统计与AI交叉领域发表SCI/SSCI学术期刊、会议、著作20余篇，成果见于Journal of Forecasting、Computational Economics、International Review of Finance、Information and Software Technology等主流国际期刊；开发的开源经济统计分析R工具包[dfvad](https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/dfvad/index.html)被CRAN官方收录，累计下载超1.8万次。主持/参与国家、省、市各级科研项目，统筹10余项企业横向课题，推动生成式AI评测、可解释AI、集成学习等技术成果在政务、金融、能源、通信等领域的转化应用。

成果已通过产学研落地验证：政务AI方面，主导构建政务大模型测评体系，成果获政府部门书面感谢信，并为地区数字人才调研提供支撑；经济测度方面，开展大数据背景下效率统计研究，相关方法应用于经济监测等场景并形成机构内部报告；风险治理方面，将大数据技术应用于异常检测预警、信用评价、反诈核验与财务智能分析，方案被相关垂域头部企业采纳。
