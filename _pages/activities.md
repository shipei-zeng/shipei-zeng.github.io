## Public Service

- _Science, Technology and Innovation Bureau of Shenzhen_ 深圳市科创局. This collaboration involves high-tech enterprise reports, project evaluation using text analysis, and an automated system for approving requests on R&D funds. These efforts are designed to streamline the work of the bureau and enhance government efficiency.
- _Government Services and Data Management Bureau of Shenzhen_ 深圳市政数局. An evaluation index system was developed for large language models in the field of people's livelihood appeals. The outcome was recognized by Shenzhen People's Livelihood Appeal Service Center that sent a letter of thanks to highlight the positive impact of this collaboration.
- _Talent Work Bureau of Shenzhen_ 深圳市人才工作局. This project produced the talent information LLM known as "A Shen". The results were presented at the [Shenzhen Talent Recruitment and Intelligence Introduction Activity](https://www.sribd.cn/article/971) and the [Global Innovative Talent Forum](https://www.sribd.cn/article/1017).
- _Government Services and Data Management Bureau of Longgang District, Shenzhen_ 深圳市龙岗区政数局. The collaboration here includes research on building portraits based on human-room data, predicting and identifying hidden fire risks, and data governance for raw information. Special sessions on financial lectures were also part of this initiative.
- _Education Bureau of Longgang District, Shenzhen_ 深圳市龙岗区教育局. This project focused on the data application research of students' non-cognitive ability profiles. It explored how big data and econometric models can be applied to enhance the teaching plans for students' non-cognitive abilities, aiming to improve educational outcomes.
